About This Brain Type Test

How the Brain Type Test Works

Ten either-or choices capture preferences such as planning versus acting, novelty versus familiarity, and intuition versus research. Each answer adds weight to related profiles; the highest combined score determines the result.

Understanding Your Brain Type

The eight profiles are reflection prompts, not fixed identities. Compare the result with real situations and keep only the observations that help you make a better choice.

What This Quiz Does Not Measure

This entertainment quiz does not scan your brain, measure dopamine, diagnose a condition, or replace psychological or medical advice.
