About This Brain Type Test

How the Brain Type Test Works

The Brain Type Test presents you with 10 rapid-fire decision scenarios, each with a 3-second timer. Your instinctive responses activate simulated neural pathways that map your cognitive style. The test analyzes patterns across creativity, logic, empathy, intuition, and drive to classify you into one of 8 distinct brain types.

Understanding Your Brain Type

Each brain type represents a unique cognitive fingerprint. Whether you are a Creator who sees the world through imagination, an Analyzer who thrives on data, or a Dynamo who acts on instinct, your result reveals how your mind naturally processes decisions. Understanding your brain type can help you leverage your cognitive strengths in work, relationships, and personal growth.
